# 📄 Research Paper Tracking & Summaries

## 1. Introduction to Reading Papers

**Tips for reading ML papers effectively:**
1. **Pass 1 (The Bird's Eye View):** Read the title, abstract, introduction, section headings, and conclusion. Do not read the math. 
2. **Pass 2 (The Grasp):** Read carefully, look at the figures and graphs. Understand the main idea and contributions.
3. **Pass 3 (The Deep Dive):** Re-create the paper from memory. Trace the math and read the code implementation.

## 3. Reading Template

When reading a new paper, consider these questions:
1. What is the problem the paper is trying to solve?
2. What are the key contributions?
3. What is the methodology or architecture proposed?
4. What are the results compared to the baseline?
5. What are the limitations?
